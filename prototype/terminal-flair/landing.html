  • turox

    active

    A chess engine, forged in Rust.

    A bitboard chess engine built for measured performance work. Its move generation, checked against perft, runs at about 52 million positions a second, and search runs at about 1.5 million nodes a second. A change to playing strength is kept only if an SPRT match shows it helps. It plays as a bot on Lichess, rated about 2000 in bullet and blitz.
