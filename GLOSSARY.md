# Portfolio

Carson Price's personal site at cdprice.dev: a marketing site that presents him
as an AI/ML engineer and grows into a home for his projects and writing.

## Pages

**Landing page**:
The single page at the site root, written for any reader, that introduces
Carson and points to his featured Projects.
_Avoid_: Home page, index, splash

**Project page**:
A page about one Project, for engineers who want to dig in; a Project has one
only when its page has been written.
_Avoid_: Detail page, case study

**Markdown twin**:
The plain-markdown copy of a Project page, served beside it for agents.
_Avoid_: md version, raw page

## Content

**Project**:
A piece of Carson's own work presented on the site, with its own source of
truth (usually a public repository).
_Avoid_: Repo, item, work

**Project card**:
A Project's summary on the Landing page, written for a general reader.
_Avoid_: Tile, preview

**Key figure**:
A headline value near the top of a Project page (usually a number, sometimes a
word), true as of the page's last update, that links to the part of the page
backing it.
_Avoid_: Stat, metric, highlight

**Also line**:
The single line on the Landing page that names lesser Projects without giving
them a Project card; a named Project with a Project page links to it.
_Avoid_: Honorable mentions, other projects

**Post**:
A dated piece of long-form writing, optionally about one Project.
_Avoid_: Article, blog entry, write-up

**Changelog entry**:
A short dated note recording a notable change to one Project that has a
Project page.
_Avoid_: Update, release note, news

**Profile**:
The single record of who Carson is and how to reach him (name, role, location,
photo, intro, a photo of his dog, contact and profile links), shared by every
page.
_Avoid_: Bio, about, site config

**Public resume**:
The variant of Carson's resume that the site links to, reachable by anyone,
listing only the site's contact address and no phone number.
_Avoid_: CV, resume PDF

**Application resume**:
The variant of Carson's resume he hands over directly, carrying his personal
email and phone number; never published on the site.
_Avoid_: Private resume, full resume

## Presentation

**Social preview**:
The title, description, and image that link unfurlers and search results show
for a page.
_Avoid_: OG card, link preview, meta

## Publishing

**Preview**:
A build of an unmerged branch at its own address, hidden from search engines.
_Avoid_: Staging, preview deployment

**Outage**:
A period during which the Landing page or the Public resume is not served
correctly at cdprice.dev, from the first failed check to the first passing one.
_Avoid_: Downtime, incident
